Convert to MP3

Turn WAV, FLAC, M4A, OGG and other files into MP3s, so they play on any phone or app. Title, artist, cover art and lyrics are copied over, and favorites, playlists and play counts move to the MP3.

Checking your library…

To pick songs yourself: Organize → Library, search notmp3, tick the ones you want, then Convert to MP3…

Lyrics auto-sync

AURA listens to a song with a speech model on this PC and times your lyrics to the singing — works on unreleased songs and your own music too. Nothing is uploaded. The model downloads once, the first time you use it.

Accuracy

In the batch, songs it's confident about are saved straight away; the rest are listed here for you to check. Heavily autotuned or mumbled parts can still be off — you can fix any line in the sync editor.

Discord status

Show "Listening to song by artist" on your Discord profile while music plays. Discord has to be open on this PC.

Discord Application ID

One-time setup: go to discord.com/developers/applications, click New Application, name it AURA (that's the name your friends see), and copy its Application ID here. Optional: under Rich Presence → Art Assets, upload a picture named aura to show next to the song.
